I · The Story
1923

A house that remembers
a whole century

For over a hundred years this house has stood on the slope above Progled. The writer Vasil Dimitrov — Yantai Kavalov — lived and worked here. Walls of Rhodope stone, petrol-green window shutters with carved hearts. Every detail here is precise and carries meaning. The past is not a museum — it is alive and warm; here, history has been built upon history.

IV · The Rhodope

The mountain that heals

The hostess returns from the forest with an armful of herbs… Here the guest does not simply spend the night. Here you feel, you sense, you remember the knowledge passed down through generations. You taste dishes with fragrant spices, aromatic tea and the stories of the family.

Only when the root is full, deep and strong — only then is the crown of the tree grand and majestic.

Here is what ripens around us:

Elderflower · a sparkling drink

Fermented in a glass jar with lemon, honey and water, in the sun for 4–5 days. The result is a pleasantly sparkling drink, finished with crushed mint. Refreshing and alive.

refreshingfermentedsummer
Nettle · suritsa

Fermented the same way, without lemon — the so-called suritsa our forebears used to make. Dried, it becomes flour; the seed, the root, the whole plant is a treasury.

Field horsetail · silica

A powerful herb, rich in silica. Prepared as a decoction and an infusion — picked right now, at the start of summer. The hostess gladly shares the recipe and how to dry it in the shade.

All year round

Snow and embers

The snow lingers for months. Days pass on the slopes of Pamporovo and Chepelare, evenings in the sauna and by the fireplace. The firewood stacked under the windows awaits its fire.

Herbs and coolness

The mountain wakes with the Rhodope lily from the Red Book, blooming between June and July. Long walks, herb picking, barbecues in the garden and over 300 sunny days a year.

VII · The Happy Family

One brand. One family.
Two ends of Bulgaria.

Happy House is not just this house. Behind the name of the Happy House stands one family that is three rays — each with its own address, its own team and its own story, yet the signature is one — cosiness, style and individuality.

The guest house is in the Rhodope village of Progled. The other two rays — the family restaurant Bistro and Happy Farm, a farm with animals and attractions for children — are far from here, in Varna. If you are headed that way, the home of the Happy House awaits you there.
