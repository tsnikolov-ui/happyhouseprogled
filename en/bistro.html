I · The Atmosphere
The Rooms

Rooms with moods

Every room is arranged like a chapter of an old novel — petrol-green walls with fringed chandeliers, dusty-rose rooms with felt hearts on the tables, window nooks with tulle curtains. Sit somewhere different and dinner is different.

The long table

A place for the whole family

Beneath the wicker lampshades stands the long table — for birthdays, christenings and Sunday lunches where nobody hurries. Napkins in pink and blue, fresh flowers and porcelain behind the sideboard glass.

The Garden

Under the pergola, under the trees

Behind the house hides a pergola garden overgrown with greenery — the bistro’s summer dining room. Weddings, birthdays and long candlelit evenings take place here. Ask about private events — the garden transforms for every occasion.
