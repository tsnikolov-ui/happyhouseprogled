I · Hosts with Hooves
The Mascot

Rosko, the alpaca

The fluffy symbol of calm and harmony. Rosko reminds us to slow the pace, to smile and to treasure the beauty of small moments. He is usually right there, by the treehouse — always ready to check whether you have something to eat… for him too.

The Garden

A small world behind the big gate

You pass through the wooden gate and the city stays outside. A pond with water lilies, a treehouse with a rope bridge, a green lawn and nooks under the trees. Perfect for meeting friends, a family outing or simply a small escape from the city.

The Kitchen

Fresh fish and something sweet

The grill is on, and our fresh, deliciously prepared fish is simply “mmm” — you are sure to come back for seconds and thirds. To finish: something sweet for young and old, and something cold to cool off on hot days.

II · The Farm

Mini petting zoo

Rosko and company await the little guests. Feeding the animals is part of the visit — with smiles on both sides of the fence.

For the kids

Treehouse

With a rope bridge, swings and hideouts among the branches. While the grown-ups have lunch, the child plays, makes new friends and feels at ease — mum is close by.
